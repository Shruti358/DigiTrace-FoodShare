## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, institutional software environment engineered for commercial kitchens, corporate facilities, and municipal food systems. The operational tone is pragmatic, data-first, and accountable. It intentionally departs from decorative SaaS trends by rejecting frosted glass, decorative gradients, and playful metaphors in favor of crisp visual hierarchy, high density, and operational clarity.

### Aesthetics & Tone
- **Precision & Governance:** The visual structure models an auditable ledger. Information must feel verifiable, structured, and institutional.
- **Ecological Purpose:** Visual emphasis is grounded in measured resource stewardship rather than consumer eco-trends. Emerald and deep forest greens provide immediate operational feedback for diverted waste and positive environmental yield.
- **Industrial Durability:** Designed for high-frequency workflows on desktop monitors, wall-mounted prep tablets, and scanning docks under harsh task lighting. High contrast ratios (WCAG AAA for text, AA for functional controls) ensure immediate scanability.

## Colors

The palette balances clean utility with unambiguous semantic signals. The foundation relies on a calibrated cool-slate scale that prevents visual fatigue during all-day monitoring while ensuring crisp separation between actionable cards and structural canvas layers.

### Surface & Neutral Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** System background providing contrast against pure white cards.
- **Card & Container Base (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity interactive panels and data modules.
- **Subtle Fill (`#F1F5F9`):** Inset table headers, input background hover states, and inactive tab tracks.
- **Structural Stroke (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px hairline delimiters for cards, rows, and dividers.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep navy-slate for headlines, numerical metrics, and active states.
- **Text Secondary (`#334155`):** Subheadings and structural labels.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Metadata, table captions, and helper annotations.

### Functional & Semantic Roles
- **Primary Emerald (`#059669` / Hover `#047857` / Inset `#ECFDF5`):** Primary interactions, diversion gains, composted streams, and verified carbon offsets.
- **Secondary Navy (`#0F172A` / Hover `#1E293B`):** Global navigation, primary export triggers, and structural commands.
- **Tertiary Sky (`#0284C7` / Inset `#F0F9FF`):** Water conservation metrics, fluid mass conversions, and tracking analytics.
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B` / Inset `#FEF3C7` / Text `#B45309`):** Imminent shelf-life expiration, threshold warnings, and pending review flags.
- **Critical Rose (`#EF4444` / Inset `#FEF2F2` / Text `#B91C1C`):** Spoilage events, contamination logs, and compliance violations.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on `Inter` to deliver systematic clarity across dense data layouts, analytics summaries, and configuration screens.

### Typographic Rules
- **Strict Weight Hierarchy:** Limit weights strictly to `400 (Regular)` for body/narrative text, `500 (Medium)` for interactive controls and table cells, and `600 (Semibold)` for headers, KPIs, and status badges. Never use lightweight (`100–300`) or black (`800–900`) weights.
- **Tabular Figures for Quantitative Data:** All monetary, weight (kg/lbs), volume, and timestamp columns must enforce OpenType `tnum` (tabular numbers) to guarantee vertical alignment across multi-row data tables.
- **Monochrome Display:** Headlines must not use accent colors. All headlines are rendered in deep slate (`#0F172A`) to preserve executive authority and avoid visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8px grid foundation (with 4px micro-steps for compact component internals like form inputs and badges).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fluid 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and fixed 32px (`2rem`) canvas outer margins. Max content container caps at 1600px for ultra-wide command monitors.
- **Tablet / Prep Display (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Sidebar navigations collapse into an icon rail.
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px margins. Metric grids collapse to single-column or 2-column stacked tiles.

### Spacing Principles
- Density is prioritized for enterprise efficiency. Component-to-component vertical rhythm strictly maintains 16px (`1rem`) or 24px (`1.5rem`) gaps.
- Complex data forms use a 2-column or 3-column structured alignment, ensuring labels directly sit 6px above their corresponding control.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established through a crisp, structural approach combining subtle hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) with faint, diffused ambient shadows. High-blur, colorful, or glassmorphic drop shadows are forbidden.

### Elevation Layers
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, zero shadow. Hosts background controls and inactive regions.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Tables):** Surface color `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for all standard dashboard panels, summary blocks, and list containers.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Select Menus, Tooltips):** Surface color `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Panels):** Surface color `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a non-blurred scrim of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses a disciplined medium-rounded radius that maintains software rigor without feeling sharp or harsh.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Cards, Modals, Panels:** 8px to 10px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), creating unified structural boundaries.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** Uniform 8px radius (`0.5rem`).
- **Inner Nested Elements:** Secondary nested tiles within cards must use 6px radius to avoid optical distortion against the outer 8px boundary.
- **Exceptions (Pill / Full Round):** Strictly limited to compact categorical and status badges (e.g., `Composted`, `Critical`), which utilize fully rounded ends (`rounded-full` / 9999px) with tight padding to distinguish them from actionable buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#059669`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `500`. Hover `#047857`. Focus ring `2px #059669` with `2px offset`. Height `40px` (desktop standard), horizontal padding `16px`.
- **Secondary (Slate):** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover `#1E293B`. For primary data exports and global settings triggers.
- **Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#334155`. Hover background `#F8FAFC`, hover text `#0F172A`, hover border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#334155`. Hover background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Status badges use pill geometry (`rounded-full`), `11px` semibold uppercase or capitalized typography (`label-sm`), with `6px` horizontal padding and `2px` vertical padding.
- **Logged:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Donated:** Background `#F0F9FF`, text `#0369A1`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`.
- **Composted:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
- **Expired / Spoilage:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- **Imminent Risk (Warning):** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `40px`, radius `8px`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, font size `14px`, text `#0F172A`.
- Placeholder color `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: border color `#059669`, shadow `0 0 0 1px #059669`.
- Error state: border color `#EF4444`, focus ring `#EF4444`. Includes inline error message in `#B91C1C` at `12px` font size.

### Data Tables
- Header row: background `#F8FAFC`, bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, uppercase `11px` semibold labels in `#64748B`.
- Body rows: height `52px`, bottom border `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Hover background `#F8FAFC`.
- Numerical metrics right-aligned with monospace/tabular digits (`tnum`).

### Cards & Summary KPI Blocks
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `8px`. Padding `20px` (`1.25rem`).
- KPI structure: Small category header (`12px`, `#64748B`), bold numeric value (`28px`, `#0F172A`, `data-metric`), followed by an inline trend tag (+/- % vs last period in emerald or rose).

### Modals
- Max-width `560px` (standard entry) or `800px` (batch waste audit).
- Header: `20px` semibold, border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `20px`.
- Footer: Actions right-aligned with standard `8px` gap, border-top `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#F8FAFC`, padding `16px 20px`.

### Toasts & Notifications
- Compact banner attached top-right or bottom-right.
- Border `1px solid`, left accent bar `4px solid` matching semantic intent (Emerald for audit log success, Rose for batch error).
- Background `#FFFFFF`, shadow level 2.
